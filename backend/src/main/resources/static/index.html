How BorrowBox Works

Simple, secure, transparent rental workflow for renters and owners

1. Discover & Check Dates

Search equipment by category, location, or price. Select rental start and end dates with real-time availability check.

2. Book & Approve

Submit your request with transparent pricing and refundable deposit. Owner reviews and approves your booking.

3. Inspect & Handover

Record equipment condition with photos before pickup. Enjoy using the gear for your production or event.

4. Return & Deposit Refund

Return equipment to owner. Owner verifies item condition, completes rental, and security deposit is released.
